Fast, small, and out of your way.

Pith is a keyboard launcher for the Mac. Press ⌘ P from any app, type a few letters, press ⏎. Apps, files, folders and everything you copied, in a native app that uses 44 MB and no CPU while it waits.

Everything is a key away.

No Dock icon, no menu bar icon, no window to click. These are all the keys there are.

Point at a lit key, or press one.

  • ⌘POpen or close Pith, from any app.
  • ⌘⇧VYour clipboard history, from any app.
  • ⌘KThe selected item’s actions, or back to the search.
  • ⌘Q…UIn ⌘K, run the first to seventh action.
  • ↑↓Move the selection, or ⌃J and ⌃K.
  • ⏎Open it, run the action, or paste the clip where you were.
  • escBack one level, or close.
  • spaceWith an empty search, Quick Look the selected file. Or ⌘Y.
  • ⌫On the clipboard’s empty search, back to the launcher.
  • cspaceTyped in the launcher, switches to the clipboard.

Fast

Built when your Mac starts and searched in memory: about 2 ms of work per keystroke, over 35,000 files.

Small

44 MB of memory and 0 % CPU while it waits, measured on my Mac today.

Private

No account, no telemetry, no AI. It goes online only to check for updates.

Native

Swift and AppKit, light or dark with your Mac, and set up in one text file.

Apps, files and folders in one list.

Type a few letters, in any order: “acme deploy” finds deploy.sh in your Acme folder. The row you want is usually the first, because Pith learns what you pick.

Your folders stay indexed as files come and go, and long names are cut in the middle, so you still see where they end.

Every action on ⌘ K.

Reveal in Finder, copy the path, open with another app, Quick Look, zip, move to the Trash, quit or force quit.

⌘ Q to ⌘ U run the first seven straight away, and Force Quit waits for a second ⏎.

Everything you copied, a ⌘ ⇧ V away.

Text, links, images and files, with a preview big enough to know it at a glance. ⏎ pastes it into the app you were in.

Pin what you reuse. The rest stays on your Mac for 30 days, up to 2 GB, and copies from password managers are never recorded.

One text file. No settings window.

Hotkeys, folders, your terminal, which app opens what. Save the file and the change is live.

A mistake shows up as a row at the top of the launcher, and ⏎ opens the file.

Also: Quick Look with Space · Zip in the background · recent files, running apps or nothing when it opens · starts at login · updates itself, or Check for Updates when you want.

Why I made it

I used Raycast, and I think they did a great job. But all I ever needed was the launcher. I don’t need most of the tools, I’m tired of AI being put into everything, and RAM isn’t something I can hand out freely to my programs anymore.

So Pith is a launcher for people who need a launcher, and nothing else.

Opening it the first time

Pith isn’t notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you once to trust it.

  1. Download and move it

    Unzip Pith.zip and drag Pith into your Applications folder.

  2. Open Anyway

    Open Pith. When macOS blocks it, go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and press Open Anyway.

  3. Press ⌘ P

    That’s it. To paste from the clipboard history, allow Pith under Accessibility when it asks.

Needs macOS 26 Tahoe or later and Apple Silicon. Pith takes ⌘ P from every app, Print included; you can change it in the config. Updates arrive on their own.
